Brand · line work

Thin lines, one soft gradient.

Every motif comes from the cue mark: five circles, a queue, rounds of work, the overlap between a studio and its client. Lines are drawn, never filled. One shape per motif carries a gradient, and that shape is the point: the current round, the cue, the agreement.

Use it

For brand moments: sign in, invites, empty states, onboarding, marketing.

One motif per view, sized to sit beside a headline, not under data.

Don’t

Fill a shape solid, or use two gradient colors in one motif.

Thicken lines past 1.5px, or add shadows to them.

Put line work on tables, charts or forms. Working screens stay quiet.
